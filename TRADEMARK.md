# Trademark policy

The code of Gryffi is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The name
"Gryffi" and the Gryffi logo are not: they belong to Gryffi, a trade name
registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number
80453783. This policy says how you can use them.

The goal is simple: people should always know whether they are using Gryffi
as Gryffi makes it, or something else.

## You can, without asking

- Run Gryffi for your own organization, changed or not, with the name and the
  logo as they come with the software.
- Say that your product or service works with Gryffi, or is based on Gryffi.
- Write about Gryffi, review it, teach it, and link to gryffi.com or this
  repository.
- Use the logo to link to Gryffi, without changing its shape or colors.

## Ask first

- Offering Gryffi to other organizations, for example as a hosted service,
  under the name Gryffi.
- Using "Gryffi", or a name that looks or sounds like it, in the name of your
  product, service, company or domain.
- Selling merchandise with the name or the logo.
- Anything that suggests Gryffi made, supports or endorses your product.

## Changed versions

If you distribute a changed version of Gryffi to others, give it a name of its
own and say that it is based on Gryffi. Replace the Gryffi logo in the parts
people see, unless we agreed otherwise. The AGPL-3.0 still applies to the code:
this policy limits only the use of the name and the logo.

## Questions

Not sure whether your use is fine? Email info@gryffi.com. We would rather help
you find a way that works than say no.
