# Magic Links

Participants do not need a password. They open a journey with a personal link in their invitation email: the magic link. Public journeys work with a public link instead.

See: public-journeys

## Overview

Magic links enable:

- Passwordless access for participants
- One personal link per participant and journey
- Continuing later with the same link

[[goto:/dashboard/invitations]]

## How Long a Link Works

You choose how long the link works when you send the invitation: 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month or 3 months.

See: invitation-options

The participant can use the link as often as they like within that period. How often they can complete the journey depends on the invitation option "How many times can the journey be completed?".

## Devices

A participant can open their link on two devices without extra steps. On a third device, Gryffi asks for a verification code. The code is sent to the participant's email address and works for 10 minutes.

## When the Link Does Not Open the Journey

| Situation | What the participant sees |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| The link has expired | "Link expired", with the button **Ask for a new link** |
| The invitation was revoked | "Invitation withdrawn" |
| The journey is completed as often as allowed | "Journey completed" |

### Ask for a new link

On an expired link, the participant can click **Ask for a new link**. Gryffi then emails the maker who sent the invitation. If that maker is no longer active, all admins of the organization get the email. The participant can ask once per 24 hours.

The participant does not get a new link automatically. Send them a new invitation under Invitations.
