# Trash

A journey or AI guide you delete goes to the trash first. From the trash you can restore it, or delete it for good.

Only makers with the role Admin or Journey Manager can move journeys and AI guides to the trash.

## Delete a Journey

1. Go to **Journeys**.
2. Open the menu of the journey and click **Move to Trash**.
3. Confirm with **Move to Trash**.

A published journey cannot go to the trash. Click **Take offline** first. A journey that is used as a sub-journey in another journey cannot go to the trash either: remove it from that journey first.

Before you confirm, Gryffi shows what happens to the journey. It lists the active invitations and the claimed reward codes. Claimed codes stay valid. Unclaimed codes are cancelled. If the journey has polls with votes, Gryffi lists them. Polls are always archived, so their results stay visible in Analytics.

[[goto:/dashboard/journeys]]

## Delete an AI Guide

1. Go to **AI guides**.
2. Open the menu of the guide and click **Delete Guide**.
3. Confirm.

A guide that is used in a journey cannot be deleted. Remove it from those journeys first.

## Restore From the Trash

1. Go to **Journeys** or **AI guides**.
2. Click **View Trash**.
3. Click **Restore** next to the journey or guide.

A journey or guide in the trash cannot be edited. Restore it first.

[[goto:/dashboard/journeys/trash]]

## Delete for Good

In the trash, **Delete Permanently** removes a journey or guide right away.

A journey that has results keeps those results in Analytics: progress, quiz scores, poll votes, form submissions and their files, rewards and video statistics. The journey itself cannot be restored anymore. A journey without any results is removed completely.

See: analytics-overview

Gryffi also empties the trash by itself. The trash shows the date on which each item is deleted for good. In the Community Edition, items stay in the trash for 30 days. After that they are deleted for good.
