# Trash

A journey or AI guide you delete goes to the trash first. From the trash you can restore it, or delete it for good.

## Delete a Journey

1. Go to **Journeys**.
2. Open the menu of the journey and click **Move to Trash**.
3. Confirm with **Move to Trash**.

A published journey cannot go to the trash. Click **Take offline** first. A journey that is used as a sub-journey in another journey cannot go to the trash either: remove it from that journey first.

Before you confirm, Gryffi shows what happens to the active invitations, rewards and poll results of the journey. You choose whether poll results are archived (they stay visible in Analytics) or deleted.

[[goto:/dashboard/journeys]]

## Delete an AI Guide

1. Go to **Guides**.
2. Open the menu of the guide and click **Delete Guide**.
3. Confirm.

A guide that is used in a journey cannot be deleted. Remove it from those journeys first.

## Restore From the Trash

1. Go to **Journeys** or **Guides**.
2. Click **View Trash**.
3. Click **Restore** next to the journey or guide.

A journey or guide in the trash cannot be edited. Restore it first.

[[goto:/dashboard/journeys/trash]]

## Delete for Good

In the trash, **Delete Permanently** removes a journey or guide right away. This cannot be undone.
