# Reward Tracking

In Analytics, the tab **Rewards** shows the rewards of your Reward steps. You see how many codes participants claimed and how many are left.

[[goto:/dashboard/analytics#rewards]]

## Reward cards

Each reward has its own card. The card shows:

- The journey the reward belongs to
- The reward title and description
- **Claimed**: how many codes participants claimed
- **Remaining**: how many codes are left
- **Valid until**: only when you set an end date
- **Redeem at** and **Terms**: only when you filled them in

How Gryffi counts **Remaining**:

- You set a maximum number of recipients: the maximum minus the claimed codes.
- Gryffi makes the codes and there is no maximum: no limit (∞).
- You added your own codes: the codes that nobody claimed yet.

When a reward has 3 codes or fewer left, the Dashboard shows it under To do.

## View codes

Click **View codes** to see all codes of a reward. For each code you see:

- **Code**
- **Status**: claimed or "Not claimed yet"
- **Claimed by**: the participant who got the code
- **Claimed at**: the date and time

## Download PDF

Click **Download PDF** to save a list of all codes of the reward. The PDF also shows where to redeem the reward, the end date and the terms, when you filled them in.

## Archived Rewards

A reward is archived when its journey goes to the trash. It is also archived when you delete a Reward step whose codes were already claimed. Claimed codes stay valid.

The section **Archived Rewards** only shows when the switch **Show deleted journeys and steps** at the top of Analytics is on. Each archived reward shows **Total claimed** and, if set, **Was valid until**. It is marked:

- **Restorable**: the journey is still in the trash. The reward comes back when you restore the journey.
- **Permanent**: the journey or the step is gone for good. Only the results are left.

You can still click **View codes** and **Download PDF** for an archived reward.
