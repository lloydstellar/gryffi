# Editor Role

The Editor role is for makers who build journeys and AI guides. Editors do not publish, do not invite participants and do not manage makers or settings.

## Overview

Editors can:

- Create and edit journeys and AI guides
- Preview journeys
- Open Analytics, including Forms, and download reports
- See the Invitations page

Editors cannot:

- Publish journeys or take them offline. Ask an admin or Journey Manager.
- Send or revoke invitations to participants
- Move journeys or AI guides to the trash
- Add or manage participants and groups
- Invite makers or change roles
- Change the settings on the **Organization** page
- Delete the results of deleted journeys and steps, or archived polls

## When to Give the Editor Role

Good for:

- Content creators
- Instructional designers
- Colleagues who build journeys that someone else checks and publishes
