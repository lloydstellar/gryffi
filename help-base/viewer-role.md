# Viewer Role

The Viewer role gives read-only access. It is for people who want to see journeys and results without changing anything.

## Overview

Viewers can:

- See all journeys, also drafts, and open them in the builder without editing
- Preview journeys
- See AI guides and chat with them to test them
- Open Analytics, including Forms, and download reports
- Change their own settings in **My account**

Viewers cannot:

- Create, edit, copy, publish or delete journeys
- Create or edit AI guides
- Open the Invitations page or send invitations
- Manage participants or groups
- Open the Makers page
- Change the settings on the **Organization** page
- Delete results in Analytics

Viewers do not see a To do list or tips on the Dashboard.

## When to Give the Viewer Role

Good for:

- **Executives** who follow the results
- **Stakeholders** who want to see progress
- **Reviewers** who check journeys before they are published
- **Consultants** who give advice
