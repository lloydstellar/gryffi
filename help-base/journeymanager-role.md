# Journey Manager Role

A Journey Manager builds and publishes journeys and invites participants. A Journey Manager does not manage makers, participants or the settings of the organization.

## Overview

Journey Managers can:

- Create and edit all journeys and AI guides
- Publish journeys and take them offline
- Make a journey public or private
- Send invitations to participants and groups that already exist, and revoke them
- Move journeys and AI guides to the trash and restore them
- Delete journeys for good from the trash
- Reset the results of a poll in the builder
- Open Analytics, including Forms, and download reports
- See the list of makers on the **Makers** page (read-only)

Journey Managers cannot:

- Add, edit or delete participants or groups, or link Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. Only admins can.
- Invite makers or change roles
- Change the settings on the **Organization** page, the API key or themes
- Delete AI guides for good from the trash
- Delete the results of deleted journeys and steps, or archived polls

## When to Give the Journey Manager Role

Good for:

- **Team leads** who decide what their team gets to see
- **Training managers** who manage learning content
- **HR managers** who run onboarding journeys
- **Content supervisors** who check and publish journeys
